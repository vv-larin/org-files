* Nikita Mishin: Education, Philanthropy and International Engagement

Since exiting his business interests in transportation and logistics, Nikita Mishin has increasingly focused on philanthropic activities around the world. His earliest structured charitable efforts began in Russia with the establishment of the Dar Foundation in 2005, created to broaden access to education and to support institutions serving children in difficult life circumstances. Through Dar, Mishin advanced programmes for talented students from disadvantaged backgrounds, supported creative and academic initiatives, and worked with orphanages and rehabilitation centres.[fn:1][fn:2][fn:3]

A major undertaking during this period was the creation of The New School (Novaya Shkola), a 600-student independent school in Moscow, launched in 2017. According to public reporting, establishing the school required an investment of almost USD 20 million.[fn:4] Mishin has expressed an educational philosophy centred on critical thinking, deep reading, engaged learning, and fostering an environment in which students learn not out of fear of mistakes but through curiosity and intellectual honesty.[fn:5] The New School continues to operate today and maintains an international-facing academic approach. According to the school’s yearly reports, on average a quarter of its graduates continue their education abroad.[fn:6]

Apart from supporting the continued operation of The New School, most of Mishin's Russian charitable activities have ceased. In recent years, he has increasingly directed his attention to international work, building a growing portfolio of educational, health, cultural, and environmental projects. His total philanthropic contributions exceed USD 47 million.[fn:7]

* Education
Mishin’s international educational work focuses on providing children from low-income families with access to quality learning opportunities, supporting their chances to complete school and, where possible, continue to university. His programmes span multiple regions and include both direct support for schools[fn:8][fn:9], student enrichment programmes[fn:10][fn:11] and broader capacity-building initiatives for teachers and communities[fn:12][fn:13][fn:14] in Africa, as well as supporting educational projects in Israel and Montenegro.[fn:3]

He has also supported international scholarship initiatives through UWC Dilijan[fn:7][fn:15], Armenia, which forms part of the broader United World Colleges network.

* Health and Humanitarian Support
Mishin also supports international health and humanitarian initiatives that assist children and families facing serious medical challenges. His contributions help improve access to treatment, supportive care, and essential services for those affected by severe illness.

Among the organisations he has supported are Podari.Life[fn:16], which provides treatment assistance for children with life-threatening conditions, and the Pancyprian Association for Childhood Cancer and Related Diseases One Dream One Wish[fn:17].

* Culture and the Arts
Mishin has a longstanding interest in cultural philanthropy. He supports several UK-based musical organisations, including the Royal Philharmonic Orchestra and the London Philharmonic Orchestra, both of which have acknowledged him among their benefactors[fn:18][fn:19], and Vache Baroque, whose website lists him as a supporter.[fn:20]

He is also listed as a donor of the Royal Academy of Music in London.[fn:21] Mishin is a long-time sponsor of a scholarship programme for its students.

He also provides support for musicians and theatre actors and directors who have been barred from performing in Russia after 2022.

* Environment and Conservation
Being a passionate fly-fisherman, Mishin has contributed to major ecological efforts aimed at preserving wild salmon ecosystems. Along with supporting river and habitat protection efforts in Russia he served for several years as a board member of the Wild Salmon Center, a US-based conservation organisation recognised for its work protecting key salmon habitats across the North Pacific.[fn:22] In 2023, the Wild Salmon Center was designated an undesirable (effectively prohibited) organisation in Russia.[fn:23]

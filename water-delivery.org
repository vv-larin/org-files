* The story
The villages of Hilltop, Riverside, and Crossroads all depend on a single water truck that travels from the town of Emthonjeni. Every day, families in these villages rely on this truck to bring enough clean water for drinking, cooking, and washing. But recently, the municipal engineers noticed a problem: the storage tanks in the villages are running low, and the truck can only carry a limited amount of water each day.\\
\\
To make matters more urgent, the truck must complete all its deliveries before sunset, and the roads between the villages are long and winding. If the water is not delivered wisely, one or more villages could run out, putting people at risk.\\
\\
You have been asked to help plan the deliveries. Using the information provided, your task is to work out how much water each village needs, how far the truck must travel, and how to design a safe 3-day rescue plan so that all three villages have enough water stored.\\
\\
Read the facts carefully, think step by step, and use your reasoning skills. The people of the three villages are counting on you.

* Background Information

** Water needs

- Each person needs 25 litres (L) of water *per day* for drinking, cooking and washing.
- A water tank in a village is considered *safe* if it has at least *2 days* of water stored.
- Even if a village starts the day with no water or not enough water (for the day), but enough water is delivered during the day, people will not suffer.
- 1 kilolitre (kL) = 1 000 L.

** The water truck

For this problem:

- The truck can carry 8 000 L of water when it is full.
- The truck always starts the day in Emthonjeni with a full tank.
- It must return to Emthonjeni before the sun sets, which is in 8 hours.
- The truck travels at a constant speed of 40 km/h on all roads.

Assume that emptying water into a village tank (any amount) takes 1 hour per stop.

Helpful formulas:

- Daily water needed in a village:
  \[ \text{Daily need (L)} = \text{Population}\times25 \]
- Time to travel:
  \[ \text{Time (hours)} = \frac{\text{Distance (km)}}{\text{Speed (km/h)}} \]

** Part D – Design a 3-day rescue plan

The municipality gives you this challenge:

#+BEGIN_QUOTE
/You have 3 days to make all three villages safe./

/At the end of Day 3, every village must have at least 2 days of water in its tank./

/You can use the water truck once per day for at most 8 hours per day./
#+END_QUOTE

Rules:

- Each morning, the truck starts *full* (8 000 L) at Emthonjeni.
- You choose which villages to visit and how much water to give each village on each day.
- You cannot give more water than the truck is carrying.
- You must respect the *time limit* each day (driving + emptying).
- You must avoid a situation where a village runs out of water. That means that the amount of water it started the day with plus water delivered that day has to at least equal its daily need.
- In maths terms:
  \[ \text{Water (morning)} + \text{Water (delivered)} \ge \text{Daily need} \]

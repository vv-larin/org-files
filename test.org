* Role Summary 
:PROPERTIES:
:UNNUMBERED: t
:END:
The Learning Center Programme Manager will lead the launch, development, and day-to-day management of Mosaic’s new Learning Center—a space designed to provide high-quality learning opportunities, academic support, enrichment activities, and holistic development for high-potential young people.
This is both a strategic and operational role. The Programme Manager will build the Learning Center from the ground up, design learning programmes, recruit and mentor a local team of educators, establish safeguarding and operational systems, and ensure excellent learning outcomes.
The ideal candidate is an education-focused leader with strong experience in setting up learning environments, building local capacity, and working in culturally diverse contexts.

* Key Responsibilities 
** Launch & Set-Up of the Learning Center
- Lead all operational preparations needed for opening the Learning Center.
- Set up learning environments, equipment, resources, safety systems, and student areas.
- Develop and implement operational policies: attendance, code of conduct, safeguarding, tutor/volunteer management, data privacy, and emergency procedures.
- Establish and maintain a culture of safety, care, and respect for all learners.

** Programme Design & Delivery
- Design the Learning Center’s core academic and enrichment programmes, including digital literacy, leadership, creativity, problem-solving, and wellbeing.
- Create term calendars, workshop modules, timetables, and assessment processes.
- Ensure programming brings together local relevance with broader international exposure, helping students engage confidently with different cultures and nationalities.
- Incorporate social-emotional learning and personalised learning pathways.

** Building & Developing a Local Team of Educators
- Recruit, train, and supervise local tutors, facilitators, and administrators.
- Provide ongoing coaching, mentorship, and professional development.
- Create opportunities for staff to learn from international educators, visiting experts, or cross-cultural collaborations.
- Foster a supportive team culture grounded in shared learning, empowerment, and student-centered practice.

** Capacity Building
- Strengthen the long-term capacity of the local team through training in pedagogy, classroom management, safeguarding, curriculum design, and learner support.
- Develop toolkits, training modules, and knowledge-sharing systems to ensure sustainability.
- Encourage innovation, reflective practice, and leadership growth among local staff.

** Student Recruitment, Support & Engagement
- Lead recruitment and selection of students for the Learning Center.
- Oversee student onboarding, orientation, and wellbeing systems.
- Build strong relationships with learners and families.
- Track individual progress and coordinate academic or pastoral interventions when needed.

** Safeguarding & Welfare
- Serve as the Learning Center’s Designated Safeguarding Lead (or oversee the DSL role if delegated).
- Ensure safeguarding policies and procedures are consistently followed.
- Conduct and update risk assessments for all programmes, activities, and spaces.
- Maintain safeguarding logs, reporting processes, and follow-up actions.

** Operations & Administration
- Manage daily operations such as scheduling, resource allocation, attendance tracking, and classroom management.
- Oversee budgets for materials, facilities, and staffing.
- Maintain operational documentation and learner data systems.

** Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning (MEL)
- Create and implement systems to track academic progress, engagement, wellbeing, and overall programme effectiveness.
- Produce monthly and quarterly reports for internal leadership.
- Use data and feedback to improve programmes and inform long-term planning.

** Community & Stakeholder Relations
- Build partnerships with local schools, educators, community organisations, and volunteers.
- Organise events, learning exchanges, and collaborative initiatives that foster cross-cultural understanding and broaden students’ horizons.
- Represent Mosaic’s Learning Center in community and educational networks.

* Person Specification 
** Essential NOOOOO
- 5+ years’ experience in education, youth development, or programme management.
- Experience launching or running a learning center, school, academy, or education programme.
- Strong skills in curriculum design and experiential learning.
- Experience developing and mentoring local teams and building institutional capacity.
- Proven ability to work effectively with young people.
- Strong safeguarding knowledge and readiness to serve as DSL.
- Excellent organisational, leadership, and communication abilities.
- Able to manage multiple priorities in a dynamic environment.
- Committed to youth wellbeing, inclusion, and ethical practice.

** Desirable AAAA
- Experience in international or cross-cultural education environments.
- Training or experience in teacher development and coaching.
- Familiarity with MEL systems or data-driven programme design.
- Experience in community engagement or partnership building.
- Knowledge of project budgeting and operations.
